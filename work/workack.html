AI-powered HR and payroll for global teams.

Workack is an HR and payroll platform used by teams across the UK, UAE, US and India. It covers the employee lifecycle — records, onboarding, leave and payroll runs — in one place.

The platform runs on a PHP and MySQL core with AJAX-driven screens, and I work on it as a full-stack developer at Neoera Infotech.
